A network for the work between people.

The chain is infrastructure for shared worlds: where a game item, a song, a contribution or an idea can carry its history forward.

We are building a place where ownership has context, creation has provenance, and communities can coordinate without losing the thread.

The first release is taking shape around EVM compatibility, open tooling and a practical path from prototype to public network.
